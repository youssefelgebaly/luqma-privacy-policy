Privacy Policy

Your food and wellness journal belongs to you. This policy explains what information stays on your device and what is shared only when you choose to use AI food analysis.

Effective date: November 12, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Luqma mobile application (the “App”), package name com.elgebaly.luqma. Luqma is a personal food and wellness journal that helps you record meals, symptoms, and food-related habits. It is not a medical service and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

2. Information you enter in Luqma

You may choose to enter information such as meal descriptions, food details, symptoms, habits, notes, and related journal entries. This information is stored locally on your device in the App's local database. Luqma does not operate its own backend server or user-account system, and the App does not upload this journal information to a Luqma server.

Because this information may reveal details about your diet or wellbeing, please treat it as personal information and protect access to your device.

3. Optional AI food analysis and images

Luqma may offer an optional feature that analyzes a food image using Google's Gemini AI service. This feature is not required to use the journal.

  • An image is sent to Google's Gemini service only after you choose an image and explicitly confirm that you want to submit it for analysis.
  • The image and any accompanying prompt or information needed for the analysis are transmitted to Google for processing. Do not include faces, documents, location details, or other sensitive information in an image unless necessary and you are comfortable sharing it.
  • Google processes submitted content under its applicable terms and privacy practices. Google's handling, retention, and use of submitted content may depend on the Gemini product, API configuration, and applicable terms.
  • Luqma does not control Google's systems or its independent data practices. Review Google's relevant privacy information before using the feature.
  • If you do not consent to sending an image, do not use the AI analysis feature. You can continue using the App's local journal features.

AI-generated results may be inaccurate and are provided for general informational purposes only. They are not medical advice.

4. How we use information

Information stored locally is used to provide the App's journaling features, display your entries, and show summaries or insights within the App. Content submitted through the optional AI feature is sent to Google so that Gemini can generate the requested analysis.

5. Sharing and third parties

Luqma does not sell your personal information. We do not share your locally stored journal entries with third parties through a Luqma backend because we do not operate one. The exception is content you expressly choose to submit through the optional Gemini feature, which is shared with Google for processing as described above.

Google is an independent third-party service provider. Its services are subject to Google's own terms and privacy policy. Luqma does not control how Google handles information after it is submitted to its service.

6. Storage, security, and deletion

Your journal entries are stored on your device. Their security also depends on your device's operating-system security, screen lock, backups, and who can access the device. We recommend using a device passcode and keeping your operating system up to date.

You can remove locally stored information using the App's available deletion controls, where provided, or by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. Uninstalling or clearing storage may permanently delete local journal data. Information already submitted to Google for AI processing is handled under Google's applicable policies and settings; deleting the App does not necessarily delete information held by Google.

7. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, the App may request access to device features such as photos or the camera so you can select or capture a food image. Access is used to support the feature you initiate. An image is not sent for AI analysis unless you explicitly confirm submission.

8. Children's privacy

Luqma is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a feature that sends content to a third party, please contact us so we can review the concern.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.
